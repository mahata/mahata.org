What Microsoft collects

Microsoft, as a third party, may collect personal data from visitors to this site: how the page renders and how you interact with it, such as mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling. Microsoft also collects or receives this personal data to provide Microsoft Advertising. The Microsoft Privacy Statement explains how Microsoft uses it.

Clarity assigns you a unique user ID and may use cookies to recognize you on later visits. This site does not ask for cookie consent, so Clarity sets no cookies for visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, and gives each of their page views its own ID instead.

How to opt out

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, Clarity does not record your visit. You can also opt out by selecting Microsoft on the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.
